# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include showing empathy, respecting differing viewpoints, giving constructive feedback, and accepting responsibility. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, sexualized attention, threats, publishing private information, and other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through GitHub's repository reporting facilities. Project maintainers will review reports fairly, protect reporters' privacy where possible, and may remove contributions or restrict participation when necessary.
